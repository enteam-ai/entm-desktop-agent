This interview uses monitoring software

Please read this before you agree. Nothing is recorded or sent until you do.

What it reads

  • Which applications are running
  • Which application is using your microphone
  • The title of your active window
  • Which windows are set to hide from screen sharing

What it never reads

  • Anything you type
  • What is inside your windows
  • Your screen, camera, or audio
  • Your files, clipboard, or browsing history

You see exactly what your interviewer sees. A window stays open showing the live feed as it is sent. If it is not in that window, it is not being sent.

You can stop at any time, with one click. Monitoring ends immediately and your interviewer is told that it stopped.

You can decline. The interview goes ahead either way. Your interviewer is simply told that monitoring was declined.

Ends when the interview ends.
